## 题目

$$
\alpha_1=[1,0,1]^{\mathrm T},\quad
\alpha_2=[1,2,3]^{\mathrm T},\quad
\alpha_3=[2,2,4]^{\mathrm T}
$$

$$
V=\operatorname{span}\{\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_3\}
=\{k_1\alpha_1+k_2\alpha_2+k_3\alpha_3\mid k_1,k_2,k_3\in\mathbb R\},
$$
