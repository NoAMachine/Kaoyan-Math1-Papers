# 2001 年数学一解答题第 12 题

设函数 $z = f(x,y)$ 在点(1,1)处可微，且 $f(1,1) = 1, \left.\frac{\partial f}{\partial x}\right|_{(1,1)} = 2, \left.\frac{\partial f}{\partial y}\right|_{(1,1)} = 3, \varphi(x) = f(x,f(x,x))$ 求 $\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}x}\varphi^3 (x)\Bigg|_{x = 1}.$
