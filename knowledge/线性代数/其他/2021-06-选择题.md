# 2021 年数学一选择题第 6 题

（6）已知 $\pmb{\alpha}_{1} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 0 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}, \pmb{\alpha}_{2} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 2 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}, \pmb{\alpha}_{3} = \begin{pmatrix} 3 \\ 1 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix}, \pmb{\beta}_{1} = \pmb{\alpha}_{1}, \pmb{\beta}_{2} = \pmb{\alpha}_{2} - k\pmb{\beta}_{1}, \pmb{\beta}_{3} = \pmb{\alpha}_{3} - l_{1}\pmb{\beta}_{1} - l_{2}\pmb{\beta}_{2}$ ，若 $\pmb{\beta}_{1}, \pmb{\beta}_{2}, \pmb{\beta}_{3}$ 两两相交，则 $l_{1}, l_{2}$ 依次为（ ）.

(A) $\frac{5}{2}, \frac{1}{2}$

(B) $-\frac{5}{2}, \frac{1}{2}$

(C) $\frac{5}{2}, -\frac{1}{2}$

(D) $-\frac{5}{2}, - \frac{1}{2}$
