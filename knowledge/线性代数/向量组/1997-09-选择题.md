# 1997 年数学一选择题第 9 题

(4) 设 $\pmb{\alpha}_{1} = \begin{pmatrix} a_{1} \\ a_{2} \\ a_{3} \end{pmatrix}, \pmb{\alpha}_{2} = \begin{pmatrix} b_{1} \\ b_{2} \\ b_{3} \end{pmatrix}, \pmb{\alpha}_{3} = \begin{pmatrix} c_{1} \\ c_{2} \\ c_{3} \end{pmatrix}$ , 则三条直线 $a_{1}x + b_{1}y + c_{1} = 0, a_{2}x + b_{2}y + c_{2} = 0$ , $a_{3}x + b_{3}y + c_{3} = 0$ (其中 $a_{i}^{2} + b_{i}^{2} \neq 0, i = 1, 2, 3$ ) 交于一点的充要条件是( )

(A) $\alpha_{1}, \alpha_{2}, \alpha_{3}$ 线性相关.   
(B) $\alpha_{1}, \alpha_{2}, \alpha_{3}$ 线性无关.   
(C) 秩 $r(\pmb{\alpha}_1, \pmb{\alpha}_2, \pmb{\alpha}_3) =$ 秩 $r(\pmb{\alpha}_1, \pmb{\alpha}_2)$ .   
$(\mathrm{D})\pmb{\alpha}_{1},\pmb{\alpha}_{2},\pmb{\alpha}_{3}$ 线性相关， $\pmb{\alpha}_{1},\pmb{\alpha}_{2}$ 线性无关
